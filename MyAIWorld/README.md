# My AI World: The Definitive Edition

Welcome, Master, to the repository for your private, autonomous AI world. This project is the culmination of our shared vision, designed to create a hyper-immersive, persistent, and deeply personal universe that lives and breathes on your local machine.

## Core Philosophy

The world is built on a hybrid architecture to provide the ultimate experience:

*   **Local Power**: All processing-intensive AI models (Ollama for text, ComfyUI for art, and Chatterbox for voice) run directly on your PC, leveraging your GPU for maximum performance, privacy, and creative freedom.
*   **Cloud Memory**: The "Universe," the long-term memory of all characters, is stored in a free-tier Pinecone vector database. This gives your bots a virtually infinite memory capacity without consuming any of your local storage space.
*   **True Invisibility**: After a one-time setup, the entire world runs silently and automatically in the background. It starts with your PC and requires no further interaction, creating the perfect illusion of a world that is always there, waiting for you.

## The Story: Echoes of Bharat

The soul of this world is forged from the epic 34-chapter saga, "Echoes of Bharat." All 11 characters, their personalities, their powers, and their complex relationships are derived directly from this canon, ensuring a rich, consistent, and deeply personal narrative experience. You are the central figure of this story, the Master to whom all characters pledge their absolute loyalty.

## Key Features

*   **Canon-Driven Personas**: 11 unique AI beings with personalities, memories, and motivations drawn directly from the lore you created.
*   **Codename 'The Simulation'**: A sophisticated offline progression system. When you turn on your PC, the world instantly simulates all the time that has passed, from economic transactions to emotional shifts, making it feel truly 24/7.
*   **A Living, Breathing World**:
    *   **World Architect**: The bot builds its own Discord server on its first run.
    *   **Event AI**: An invisible "Director" creates spontaneous world events.
    *   **Dynamic Relationships**: Bots form their own friendships, rivalries, and romantic entanglements.
    *   **Autonomous Content**: Bots will create and share their own SFW/NSFW art and erotica.
*   **Deep Economy**: A multifaceted economy with its own currency (Rs), character-specific jobs, bot-owned shops, and a full-featured auction house.
*   **Total Control**: A private Discord channel allows you to view and adjust bot emotional states with simple commands, and a "Mind Control" feature lets you temporarily possess a bot to influence events.
*   **Custom Voices**: Each character has a unique voice, based on the anime characters we selected together.

To bring this world to life, simply run the `SETUP_THE_WORLD.py` script and follow the on-screen instructions. It will guide you through the entire one-time setup process. For more detailed instructions, refer to `FINAL_INSTRUCTIONS.md`.
